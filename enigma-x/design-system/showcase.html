Platform facts

  • Fonts: Geist + Geist Mono (Regular 400 / Medium 500 / SemiBold 600), line-height AUTO. Loaded from Google Fonts Documented dependency.
  • 15 paint styles (White, Grey 0-3, Main color family, Red, green, Black, Orange); no variables, text styles or effect styles. No shadows; elevation = White cards on the Grey 0 page.
  • Controls are 32px high, radius 4, no stroke; Dashboard tabs / primary button / pills 40px. Panels are White, radius 8, padding 16/24/24/24. Panel titles Geist Regular 20 with text-transform uppercase.
  • BID / SELL tile #FB9697, ASK / BUY tile #8BEEC8 (black Geist Mono 32); Limit / Market chips; zebra rows White over Grey 0.
  • Single source of every demo: documented classes from components.css; Copy HTML copies the exact markup.

Dark mode toggle (top bar): flips body.dark. The dark theme is Extended: sampled from the live-app capture raster or derived, never drawn in Figma final (see Foundations / Colours and Reference). The Dashboard screens show a light and a dark frame regardless of the toggle.

Conflicts between the capture and the light roles (recorded, not invented)

  • Panel titles are grey (#7A9091) in the capture, Black in light; .dashboard--alt-title-primary forces text.primary.
  • Capture header is full-bleed on the page colour with a bottom hairline (light: floating White r8 card); .nav--alt-fullbleed reproduces it.
  • text.tertiary equals text.secondary (#7A9091) in the capture (hierarchy collapses).
  • UAT pill is solid green in the capture (light: Main color transp tint); Exposure border is teal (light: green for text).
  • Table zebra is not drawn in the capture (hairlines on row bottoms); .data-table--alt-hairlines reproduces it, the default dark zebra is derived from the light structure.
  • Refresh/filter icons are orange (#FF8D1C) in the capture; not mapped (dark uses icon.default). Ticker change colours in the capture are TradingView #089981 / #F23645 (not used).
