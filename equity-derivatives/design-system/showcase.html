Radii

Corner radii found in Section2 (use counts in the notes). Radius is never mixed: 4 for controls, 8 for cards and modals.

Spacing (observed)

Figma defines NO spacing tokens. These are the values observed in auto-layout padding / gap (see tokens.json comments).

Shadows & effects

The only drop shadows in Figma: toasts, notification cards, the open Cancel menu and the toggle thumb. Cards, panels and modals have NO shadow.

Motion

Not defined in the source.

The harvest files record no durations, easings, transitions or keyframes, and neither tokens.css nor components.css contains a transition, animation or @keyframes declaration. The platform brief forbids adding transitions that Figma does not imply (CLAUDE.md, section 12).

Related behaviour that is not motion: .skeleton is a static bar (“no animation token exists”, components.css); the Time chip countdown changes its text once per second (setInterval 1000 ms) and switches to the frozen state at 15 s and the ended state at 0 (Extended, see the Time chip card).
